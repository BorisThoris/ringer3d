## Sound effects (`sfx/`)

From Kenney's *Impact Sounds* (CC0): glass impacts (marble on marble), wood, plate,
generic and soft impacts (boundaries), and concrete, grass, wood and snow contact sounds
(the flick). Rolling sound and the ambient pad are synthesised in code.
