Ringer 3D privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026. Applies to Ringer 3D on Android (Google Play), Steam, and the web at ringer3d.modaxxx009.workers.dev.

Ringer 3D is a marble game. It has no accounts of its own, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. This page says what data the game handles and where it goes.

Kept on your device

Your player name, settings, coins, marble collection, achievements, match record and Tower progress are saved on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game (or clearing a browser's site data) deletes them.

Google Play Games (Android)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game uses it for achievements, leaderboards and Saved Games (a copy of your progress, so it follows you between devices). Google receives your achievements, scores and saved progress, linked to your Play Games profile, under Google's privacy policy. Leaderboards show your Play Games name and score to other players.

Steam

On Steam the game uses Steam for the same things (achievements, stats, leaderboards, Steam Cloud saves, friends and invites, and marbles as Steam inventory items), under Valve's privacy policy.

Online play

Online matches connect players directly to each other (WebRTC). To find each other, the game posts a short-lived room notice (room name, code, field, player count and the host's player name) to public Nostr relays, and connections use public STUN/relay servers. Players in a room see each other's player names and marbles, and the devices in a match can see each other's IP addresses, as with any peer-to-peer game. Nothing about a match is stored by us.

Invite links

An invite link contains only a four-letter room code. Sharing it uses your device's share sheet; we don't see who you send it to.

Purchases

Coin purchases, where offered, are made through Google Play or Steam. We receive a purchase receipt to credit your coins; payment details stay with Google or Valve.

The website

The game's website is served by Cloudflare, which keeps standard request logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for operating the service, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Children

The game does not ask for personal information and is not designed to collect any from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: use the developer contact email on Ringer 3D's Google Play or Steam page. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
